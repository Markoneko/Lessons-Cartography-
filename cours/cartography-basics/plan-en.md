| 3.6 | Ordering: how many steps? | Core | à revoir | Value (light to dark) orders, but the eye only tells a limited number of steps apart: about 2 in yellow, 3 in green, 4 in blue or orange, 5 in red or purple, 6 in grey (notes of the old deck). | 3.7 | Differentiating is not ordering | Core | à revoir | Colour (hue) differentiates, it does not order: a rainbow palette on ordered data blurs the reading.
